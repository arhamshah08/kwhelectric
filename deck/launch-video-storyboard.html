Launch video — full sequence

Every beat of the recorded demo, in order: what's on screen, the timestamp in the source recording, and the actual narration spoken over it. Built for storyboarding a shorter, edited launch cut — screenshots are cropped from the real product, nothing recreated.

Source: kWh Demo Recording.mp4, 6:37, transcribed locally. Screens shown: gateway portal (edge device), consumer app (customer mobile onboarding), utility portal (Grid Intelligence).

Act 1

The pitch

0:00 – 1:10

No product on screen yet. This is the founder framing the problem and the three ways a device gets connected — worth keeping close to verbatim, it's the clearest statement of the offer anywhere in the recording.

kwhelectric.io
0:01
Cold open — the product site
Hold on this a beat before cutting to the founder. Good title-card candidate as-is.
Voiceover "Hello, this is Arham Shah, I'm the founder of kWh Electric, and this is a demonstration of our communication infrastructure — where you can talk to any legacy energy asset, any charger, inverter, battery, transformer, using standard protocols. You can take any legacy protocol and integrate it to the open standard requirements that are growing in California, Texas, India, Australia and many more."
the offer, stated
0:28 – 1:10
The three ways to connect
No new visual here in the source — reuses the gateway portal about to be shown live. In an edit this is a strong place for on-screen text: three labelled beats, "gateway," "licence," "OEM API."
Voiceover "We will start by showcasing a gateway product where you can connect any asset to a physical product, or you can issue a licence on your existing physical device, and then there's a third product where we do OEM integrations, a standard API access. The idea is we can throw out the API access today, help them bring it to the open standards tomorrow — or if they're ready for open standards, we can throw that out immediately."

The gateway, before anything is connected

Voiceover "Let's showcase the edge portal. As you can see, this is running on the edge and we have no northbound connection, which means you have a lot of potential southbound devices, a lot of different protocols. Grid Intelligence
5:10 – 5:23
One asset today, a fleet tomorrow
This is the line to keep in any cut, however short — it's the entire pitch for why the demo matters at all.
