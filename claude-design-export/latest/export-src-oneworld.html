The meadow grew scattered.

Every garden raised its own hive — its own dance, its own doors. Whole fields of nectar sat unclaimed, because no one could ask.

Then came a common dance.

One master bee, one signal — and exactly the right bees answer. One voice, many listeners, zero translators.

Work happens at the source.

The courier drinks straight from the flower and carries it home whole. Nothing skimmed, nothing lost.

Then trouble came.

A black bumblebee dove straight for the comb — and bounced off a shield that was already on. The bee next door never looked up.

Every drop counted.

Each courier docks at its own cell, and every drop is credited to the exact bee that earned it.

Home, in its own cell.

Every bee has a cell only it can open — safe inside, door sealed.

We're building this hive.

A comb where every member has its own sealed cell. One common dance instead of a hundred dialects. Work done at the source and carried home whole. A shield that's simply always on. Every drop counted, and credited to whoever earned it.
