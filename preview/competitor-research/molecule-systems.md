- **Partners strip:** EG4 Electronics · Lightsmith Energy · Enersponse · RCT Power

## 8. Takeaways for kWh Electric

- Best reference for **Open Protocol Gateway / edge execution** storytelling: Signal → Execution → Verification; cloud-to-edge progression.
- **Who we serve** hub is a clean pattern for kWh’s 3 buyer boxes (Aggregators, OEMs, Financiers) — Molecule’s “Asset Owners” + Derapi’s “Lenders” together inform financier language.
- Product mega-menu could mirror Molecule’s **named platform layers** (AERA/EMS/…) but kWh should stay to **2 products** to avoid dilution.
- Avoid copying Molecule’s heavy jargon density on first viewport; borrow the clarity of the execution-gap diagram.
- DividendVPP shows how to talk to OEMs about **earnings platforms** without becoming a VPP yourself — relevant adjacent messaging.
