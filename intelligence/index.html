What this layer covers

Intelligence applications that become practical once Integrations and Gateway make every asset visible and dispatchable.

Flexibility automation

Automated flexibility for VPPs, aggregators, and site operators — schedule, dispatch, and verify across OEM boundaries.

Asset management

Portfolio health, performance, and operational state for financiers and fleet owners without one dashboard per manufacturer.

Demand-side management

DSM and demand response program execution with canonical commands and delivery verification.

Interoperable programs

Open-standard and multi-party workflows so utilities, OEMs, and aggregators can share the same fleet safely.

How the three layers fit

  • Integrations
    Cloud APIs that connect and normalize OEM ecosystems.
  • Gateway
    Edge protocol translation and offline-ready local control.
  • Intelligence
    Applications that automate flex, DSM, assets, and interoperable programs.
